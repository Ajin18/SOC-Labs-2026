# Phishing Email Analysis

## Overview

This file contains my investigation notes and learning outcomes from completing the Phishing Email Analysis lab on LetsDefend.

The lab helped me understand how to investigate suspicious emails, identify potential phishing indicators, and analyze email headers to support phishing investigations.

## Objectives

* Understand the fundamentals of phishing email analysis.
* Examine email headers and identify suspicious indicators.
* Investigate sender information and IP addresses.
* Understand email authentication mechanisms such as SPF.
* Identify potential Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).
* Develop a structured approach to email security investigations.

## Skills Learned

* Phishing email investigation
* Email header analysis
* Sender IP address identification
* SPF header analysis
* Identification of suspicious email indicators
* IOC identification
* Security investigation and analysis

## Investigation Process

During this lab, I practiced analyzing phishing-related email information and examining email headers to understand the origin and authenticity of a suspicious message.

The investigation focused on:

1. Examining the email header information.
2. Identifying the sender’s IP address from the relevant email headers.
3. Analyzing the Received-SPF header.
4. Understanding how email authentication information supports phishing investigations.
5. Reviewing suspicious indicators to help determine whether an email may be malicious.

## Key Takeaways

* Email headers provide useful information about the path an email takes and its source.
* SPF helps verify whether a sending server is authorized to send emails for a domain.
* Suspicious sender information and authentication failures can help identify potentially malicious emails.
* Email analysis is an important skill for SOC analysts investigating phishing alerts.
* Proper investigation and documentation help support security incident response.
